Winning a round

  • Drop their health to zero, or knock them off the edge for an instant Ring Out.
  • Knock any foe into one of the two giant golden gongs for an instant Gong KO, even a boss on full health. Careful: it works on you too.
  • Heavy hits shatter pillars. Pillar shadows are where shadow fighters hide.
  • Vents open in the floor mid-round. Watch for glowing rings.
  • Four stone walls ring the middle of every arena. They block projectiles, bounce beams, and Locust can wall-kick off them.
  • Life drops: every 40 to 60 seconds a crate with a heart floats down on a parachute. Grab it first to win back a life, or half your health if you haven't lost one.
  • Each fighter has 3 lives. Lose all three and you lose the match. In the Tournament you have 3 lives and every opponent has 1.
  • Training: practise any fighter on three dummies. Energy stays full, there are no cooldowns, dummies heal themselves, and you can switch fighters from the pause menu.
  • Story Mode: every master has a five-stage campaign with dialogue: The Invitation, Trial of the Arena (the arena itself fights back), Swarm Ambush, Rival Showdown, and the Championship Finale against the Tournament Host, who fights with abilities stolen from the masters you beat. Win it to see that master's ending.
  • Tag Team: choose a primary fighter and a backup partner. Press T or Tab to swap. Your partner leaps in with an assist attack, and the tag has a 6-second cooldown. Your resting partner slowly heals. Knock out both enemies to win.
  • Wave Survival: survive endless waves of guards: fast dagger scouts, shield-bearers whose shields only break to heavy attacks (K), and stalkers who turn invisible until they strike. A master boss arrives every third wave, and every second wave the Host calls an arena event: Low Gravity, Collapsing Floor, or Relic Scramble (hold the Jade Blade for 10 seconds to clear the wave). Defeated foes drop blue energy orbs and gold tokens to spend in the Dojo. You have 3 lives.
